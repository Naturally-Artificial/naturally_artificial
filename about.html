About

Deep experience across tech, media and venture. Currently investing in early-stage founders and exploring the real impact of AI

Origin & drive

I've been starting things for as long as I can remember. Not because it's easy — but because the alternative, working on someone else's idea, never quite fit. Over 20+ years I've built companies across technology, media, and venture. One reached unicorn status. Others didn't make it. Every single one taught me something I still use

Investing thesis

I invest in people before I invest in ideas. A great founder with a mediocre market will find a way. A mediocre team with a great market will waste it. I back early-stage founders who are willing to be proven wrong fast and iterate faster

Why the show

Naturally Artificial started as a question I kept asking at dinner tables: what is AI actually doing to the way founders build companies? Not the hype — the real operational change. The best answers came from other founders. So I put them on camera
