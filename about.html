What I bring

I built companies, raised money, missed payroll, hired badly, shipped late. About twenty attempts. One became a unicorn. That's what I bring to founders now. Pattern recognition, not a framework.

Investing thesis

Europe. Early stage. AI automation, AI roll-ups, MarTech, PropTech, AutoTech.

Why the show

I also host Naturally Artificial. Founder-to-founder talk on AI disruption, raising capital, tech hubs and relocation. Strategy for a game where the rules change mid-play. Investing and interviewing are the same conversation.
