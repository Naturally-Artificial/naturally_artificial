2 years ago he said AI would leave 70% of the world without work. Now? "Probably closer to 99%"

By 2030. Birth rates fall. Growth stalls. AI and robots take every job that can be turned into an algorithm. He's calm about it: "I really don't see a crisis at all. I see a completely natural evolutionary process"

My guest is Dmitry Green. Tech entrepreneur, investor, AI evangelist. “First million” in the 90s. Says he could have been a billionaire. Now lives in Valencia and invests from his phone

People haven't changed. They travel, ride bikes, get sick. So: energy, batteries, new food. And medicine. "Pick a disease that interests you and build a unicorn around it"

I told Dmitry AI lets solo founders build prototypes fast. He wasn't impressed. "Who cares how quickly you built a prototype?" Customers and money on the table. And a seed round today in the EU? "At least $1.5-2M"

99% of jobs gone by 2030. Evolution or crisis?
