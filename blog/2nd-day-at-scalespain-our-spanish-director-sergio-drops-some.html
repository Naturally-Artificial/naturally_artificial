2nd day at ScaleSpain.

14 March 20251.88K views

2nd day at ScaleSpain. Our Spanish director, Sergio, drops some interesting facts:

Talked with 20 property managers. 20/20 were interested in understanding how AI can help them scale their business without increasing the costs. Two of them have already implemented AI chat bots but were still super interested in knowing more about voice bots for non-working hours

Talked with ~15 prop management software providers. Only 3/15 really integrated AI. The others are considering creating a marketplace for external partners, like Placy

Those 3 providers, who have already integrated AI, were categorically against working with external partners and want to develop everything in-house

Will current tech providers adapt AI agents? Is there still space for niche AI startups?

Community, vote: who has a better chance of winning the PropTech AI market?
