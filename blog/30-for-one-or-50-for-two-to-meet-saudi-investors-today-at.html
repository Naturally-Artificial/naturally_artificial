“€30 for one or €50 for two” to meet Saudi investors

Today at Web Summit Portugal, I “visited” a session Business & Investment in Saudi Arabia

Registration on Eventbrite, €30 for one or €50 for two—weird for such a biz event, but I signed up because I’m looking for partners to launch Placy Pro in UAE and Saudi Arabia

Got to the address, and… it’s an embankment with seagulls! No building, no signs, no contact info. Just me and a few other confused startuppers hanging around like we’re waiting for a bus to Riyadh

Locals said: “Ah, another fake Web Summit event? You paid “€50 for two” to meet Saudi investors? Seriously? Hahahaha!”

Lisbon truly is the capital of pickpockets 😝
