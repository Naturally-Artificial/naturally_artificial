6 months ago Nadia (producer) and I (host) started filming…

17 July 20262.24K views

6 months ago Nadia (producer) and I (host) started filming founders and investors for Naturally Artificial on YouTube. 7 films. 100 shorts. Outcome:
• 3.7M views
• 17K likes
• 24K subscribers
• €5K earned
• €20K spent
Bonus: +3K followers on my LinkedIn and Instagram

Not a unicorn scale, but I really enjoy this pet project. It gives me a direct link to great founders' minds. Kind of live MBA: which niches are investable, which jurisdictions work, how fundraising really happens etc

My biggest concern is my spoken English. Most of my guests aren't native speakers either. But YouTube is full of native speakers! Why would anyone watch our channel? Even though broken English is the lingua franca of European tech…

Your likes boost my morale ;)
