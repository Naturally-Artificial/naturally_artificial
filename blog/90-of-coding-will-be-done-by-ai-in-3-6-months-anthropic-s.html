90% of coding will be done by AI in 3–6 months (Anthropic’s CEO).

26 March 20252.48K views

90% of coding will be done by AI in 3–6 months (Anthropic’s CEO). Natural language is the new programming (OpenAI’s CEO). Founders are building full apps by just describing ideas in plain text. Projects that used to cost €50k can now be launched overnight for €500!

Yep, 3/4 might be done by AI already. We’re skeptical about the last 1/4, which is about quality & security, right? But even without that 1/4, the big shift is here:
• dev is turning into design+prompt
• startups skip hiring tech cofounders
• PMs prototype solo

The new must-have skill? Working in sync with your AI copilots. Vibe coding at scale!
