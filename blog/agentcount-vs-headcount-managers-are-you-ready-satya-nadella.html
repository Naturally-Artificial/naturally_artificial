Agentcount vs Headcount. Managers, are you ready?

Satya Nadella says Microsoft is ditching per user and moving to per agent. Instead of paying for seats, you'll pay for the little AI extras that live on your payroll, like pets with network access. Each agent gets a name, a password, a file of sins, and its own cubicle inside Microsoft 365. Identity. Audit. Security. A line on the invoice

Google is killing its biz-model with AI snippets. Microsoft sacrifices its classic B2B “per-user” licenses

💡Insight? Boards will start asking managers to track agentcount vs headcount

Here’s the thing nobody wants to say out loud. Headcount will be the body fat metric soon. A healthy dev team diet might look like: 40% engineers, 40% AI agents, 20% interns polishing prompts. Kind of
