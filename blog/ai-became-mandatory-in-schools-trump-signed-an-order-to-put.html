AI became mandatory in schools 😑

Trump signed an order to put AI into the American education system:
🔘 AI certifications for high schoolers
🔘 AI-centered trainings for the Department of Labor
🔘 Public-private partnerships to bring AI tools into classrooms
🔘 Teacher training grants focused on AI skills
🔘 New White House AI bureaucrats to guide the strategy
