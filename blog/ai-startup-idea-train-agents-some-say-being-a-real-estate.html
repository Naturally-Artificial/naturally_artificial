AI startup idea: train agents

Some say being a real estate agent is like driving a taxi—temporary and anyone can do it. Agents' turnover is huge, corporate coaches are exhausted, and real customers don't appreciate being guinea pigs 😅

Got another idea for a side project based on an AI Real Estate Assistant: Young Agent Simulator

An AI assistant like 😇Placy is good at talking with clients. She understands how they act. She can play the role of a buyer or seller to help train new agents, making the whole process cheap and easy. Plus, she can send over recommendations for students and the agency’s management about each trainee. No more stressed customers 👍
