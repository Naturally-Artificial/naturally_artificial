AI Winter ❄️🥶

AI's been hot since the '80s, not just last year. 80s marked the AI Boom with deep learning and expert systems

1980 — the first AI conference in Stanford

1980 — the first commercial AI expert system XCON automatically picked computer components based on the customer's needs

Placy picks flats and houses, so XCON could be her grandpa. And this☝️is what her avatar might have looked like in the futuristic ‘80s 😂

1981 — Japan dropped a cool $850M on AI (~$2Bn in today’s money) to create a computer that could communicate in human language

1986 — the first driverless car could move 60 km/h on traffic-free roads

Then AI Winter came in 1987–1993 when funding and interest waned

Crazy to think we already survived the AI Winter 30 years ago! 📉🫣
