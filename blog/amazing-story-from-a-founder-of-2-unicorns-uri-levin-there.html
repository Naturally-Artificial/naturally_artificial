Amazing story from a founder of 2 unicorns Uri Levin:

There was a very successful CEO in New York who was constantly ignoring the media and never gave an interview, not even one. Until one day a reporter from the New York Times calls him up and did answer. The reporter asked him for 2 minutes and as he was in a pretty good mood after a very successful year, he agreed.

The first question that the reporter asked was - how you become such a successful CEO, and the CEO replied "Two words - right decisions". Ok, but how do you know how to make those right decisions the reported asked? He replied "One word - experience!". The reporter then went deeper - how do you gain this experience? and the CEO replied "Two words - wrong decisions."

Second time entrepreneur have way higher likelihood of being successful, regardless what happened on the first journey.
