Art News from Placy 🎨

The Company Logo is Here! - a masterpiece crafted by the talented Berlin graphic designer Yury Shustsitski. Its concept, "a road to your place", perfectly encapsulates Placy's mission: guiding people to their dream homes 🏡✨

🤔 The Great Chatbot Avatar Debate - Next step, we're pondering over the ideal look for Placy's chatbot icon/avatar. Should it follow the symbolic path like ChatGPT’s logo, or should we opt for a more human-like avatar, akin to this very Placy Journal channel?

Ilya Sutskever, Chief Scientist at OpenAI, notes that over 60% of ChatGPT users greet and thank the AI as if it were a human buddy. Despite this, conversational giants like Siri, Alexa, Cortana, Bixby use abstract/futuristic symbols as their avatars. But what's the point of saying hello and goodbye to a spiraling circle? There's something psychiatric about this, don't you think? 🙂

🔬 Embarking on an A/B Testing Adventure - Well, we've decided on the company logo, but it looks like we have an exciting journey of A/B testing ahead of us to decide on the avatar for the Placy AI assistant. Abstract symbol or human face? What's your take?
