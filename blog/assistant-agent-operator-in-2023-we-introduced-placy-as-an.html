Assistant, Agent, Operator?

In 2023, we introduced Placy as an AI Assistant. In 2024, the term AI Agent gained traction, highlighting that Assistants are kind of passive while Agents are active, right?

We didn’t change Placy’s title, not to scare human real estate agents (Placy Pro subscribers), as they worry about being replaced by AI. However, the term "agent" now better reflects Placy's capabilities. She does more than just answer questions—she schedules meetings, logs info in CRMs, etc

OpenAI uses one more tech term—Operator:
Operator is a system that can autonomously perform tasks on behalf of users. Operator is an AI agent powered by the Computer-Using Agent (CUA) model, which combines vision capabilities with advanced reasoning. It can interact with websites by typing, clicking, and scrolling, mimicking human actions to complete tasks like booking reservations or shopping online. This technology allows Operator to act as a virtual assistant, simplifying digital interactions without requiring constant human oversight


It breaks my mind! Community, what should we name Placy in our pitch deck?
