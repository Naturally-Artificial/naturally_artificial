At 35, he hit the eject button on the office life, and landed a flight school in Florida that now does $4.5M a year. Andrey Borisevich is a pilot, founder of an aviation academy in Miami, and a co-pilot on the first Belarusian round-the-world flight in a single-engine plane ✈️ 🌍

We took a helicopter over the Fort Lauderdale canals (yes, I grabbed the controls myself, it’s scary!), and Andrey shared all about it: leaving Moscow with $90K in his pocket, buying a US flight school for $650K, surviving COVID by trading stocks, buying out his partner, and why he's not chilling in the Caribbean right now

Plus the questions everyone's asking:
• Will AI actually replace pilots?
• Why is aviation the most conservative industry on Earth?
• What does it really take to get a private pilot license or buy your own plane?
• And what's the real budget for flying around the world?
