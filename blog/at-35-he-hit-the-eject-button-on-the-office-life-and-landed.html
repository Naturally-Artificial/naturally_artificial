At 35, he hit the eject button on the office life, and landed a flight school in Florida that now does $4.5M a year. Andrey Borisevich is a pilot, founder of an aviation academy in Miami, and a co-pilot on the first Belarusian round-the-world flight in a single-engine plane ✈️ 🌍
