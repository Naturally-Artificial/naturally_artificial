B2B pay-per-seat model is over.

20 February 20262.42K views

B2B pay-per-seat model is over. Why?

If 10 AI agents can handle the work of 100 employees, you don't need 100 Windows/Salesforce/whatever seats anymore, right?

AI kills the headcount that operates the software. Which kills the per-seat revenue model

Token budget is the new headcount. The best developers are already burning $10k/month in compute to do the work of entire teams

Mark Cuban on the next job wave:
Software is dead. Everything will be customized to your unique utilization. Who's gonna do it? There are 33 million companies in the US

1️⃣ Pick up a vertical
2️⃣ Learn the flows
3️⃣ Become the AI team they can’t build internally
4️⃣ Increase EBITDA and multiples
5️⃣ A piece of the upside is your profit
6️⃣Show the result to investors, raise money, scale

This biz-model has been doing great in the first decades after each previous industrial revolutions
