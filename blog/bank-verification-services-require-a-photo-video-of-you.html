10 December 20242.84K views

Bank verification services require a photo/video of you holding your passport. Why not create a similar talent profiling? Here’s what we can get from a 1-minute video of a candidate:
• Language fluency & grammar
• Age (95%+ accurate)
• Emotional state / mental vibes
• Honesty via body language

💡Idea: a talent profiling app that records a video, with AI chatting briefly with candidates. Sort of like Placy for realtors, but designed for recruiters. Saves time, screens talent smarter
