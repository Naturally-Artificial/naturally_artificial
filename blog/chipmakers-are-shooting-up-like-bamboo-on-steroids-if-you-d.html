Chipmakers are shooting up like bamboo on steroids.

18 September 20242.01K views

Chipmakers are shooting up like bamboo on steroids. If you’d invested $100 twenty years ago, here’s what you’d have today:
Nvidia: $10,000
AMD: $1,000
Intel: $0… yep, about zero

Intel should be leading the AI wave, but has completely missed the boat! It’s sinking

Remember Kodak? They invented digital photography in 1975 and launched the Kodak DCS 100 camera with a 100x100 pixel CCD in 1991. Then promptly killed it off to protect their film business. Filed for bankruptcy in 2012. Classic

Takeaway? Even when a dinosaur is blocking the sun, there’s always room for a hungry competitor or a bold [AI] startup
