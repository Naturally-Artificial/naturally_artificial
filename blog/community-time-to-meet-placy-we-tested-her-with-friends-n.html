Community, time to meet Placy! 🤖🚀

We tested her with friends-n-family in March. Now she’s yours

▫️ She speaks the language you start speaking with her, takes both text and voice messages

▫️ She helps you identify what you want, offers tips on property taxes, migration, investing. In this pilot phase, she deals with only buying real estate in Cyprus

▫️ You get relevant listings, and she helps you set up viewings with agents and landlords. Remember, we're still working on this feature – right now, Placy only covers about 1/3 of the market, but we add new data sources every week

Choose a role in your mind (from which country you are, approx budget, etc.) and… Ta da ta dam 🥳 go:

✈️ Placy

We know you might have high expectations, but remember our sweet Placy is just 4 month old. If you notice a mistake in her facts (it's a common problem of GPTs), just prompt her saying "Placy, tell your experts that…” and our team will look into it

Well, go ahead and tell us what you think of our MVP in the comments
