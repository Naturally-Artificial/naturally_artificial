Crypto is at the bottom now.

15 July 20261.53K views

Crypto is at the bottom now. Capital moves to AI. Investors, devs, analysts convinced it's over. Most L1 tokens are dying. Bitcoin and Ethereum haven't hit the floor yet. Funeral? Wrong reaction

3 areas are growing:
• Stablecoins. Already processing huge transaction volumes, still treated as a niche instrument
• Tokenization. We're at the very start of a long trend. Most real-world assets will end up trading on-chain — faster, cheaper, no middlemen. The GENIUS Act is in the Senate right now
• AI agent payments. Millions of agents will need to pay for things, APIs, compute, each other. Crypto is the only infrastructure that fits here. This market is essentially at zero. CoinDesk ran a piece on it just last week: "the real multi-trillion-dollar future of crypto is building infrastructure for machines"

Disillusionment is a signal to buy

What are you watching?👇
