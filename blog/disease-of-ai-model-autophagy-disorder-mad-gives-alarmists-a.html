Disease of AI, Model Autophagy Disorder (MAD), gives alarmists a reason to relax: human extinction seems unlikely

MAD is what happens when AI models train on data generated by other models. It degrades without fresh human data (example)

So look, if AGI aims to rule the world, it needs humans (Matrix-style?) to keep the vibe fun. Otherwise, it'll just end up eating itself and forget all about robot world domination 😑😡😀
