Each ChatGPT update wipes out another few thousand AI startups…

20 November 20252.62K views

Each ChatGPT update wipes out another few thousand AI startups like bugs on a windshield. Even Google and Wikipedia are looking for new business models. Now social messaging is shaking. OpenAI launched a pilot

Why? Frequency I guess. An average active user sends:
WhatsApp ~40 messages/day
Telegram ~30
ChatGPT ~3

So now the chatbot wants to be your messenger. Why not? The knife becomes a sword: same UX, same contacts list, more data, stickier users, larger market share. Welcome to the elegant little invasion 🤫
