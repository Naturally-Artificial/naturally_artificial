First Stats 📊

Yesterday, Placy chatted with 23 people, sent 289 messages—86 of those were property listings, plus a little nudge to 7 sleepy users 💬

Good News: As MVP, Placy's smashing our expectations—kudos to the dev team 🏆👏
Bad News: An average chat's costing up to €0.5/day per user. Shit! 💩

It’s more than expected

Chatting = burning OpenAI tokens. More chats, bigger dent in our modest startup wallet. So, a month-long socialisation might cost €15/user. Imagine when we hit tens of thousands of users. We’ll definitely optimise this later, but still… scary

A real estate lead costs €20-150. But, what about AI startups in the selling socks or pet food? Sadly, with current token prices, breaking even remains a pipe dream 💸 Thinking of launching your AI venture in these sectors? Forget
