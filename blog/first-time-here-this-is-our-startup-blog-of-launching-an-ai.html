First time here?

02 August 20242.62K views

First time here? 👀 This is our startup blog of launching an AI Real Estate Assistant, 9-month young Startup of the Year proudly part of Microsoft Startup Hub

Our core team are former colleagues from a large PropTech company that became a unicorn in 2021. Sergey Osipov, one of the founders, talks to you

😀Placy Pro is a SAAS b2b tool for real estate pros (placy.ai/pro). Placy Assistant is an AI companion for sellers and buyers

Feel free to chat with Placy Assistant prototype 🤖 Only Cyprus property is available for buying at the current early stage (no rentals yet) — don't expect much, please, the product is in the R&D phase

The team is currently focusing on Placy Pro that was showcased at the RealtyON Expo in July. 30+ potential clients are excited to integrate our AI tool, so that’s what we are doing now

We're kicking off the pilot in Cyprus. Athens, Dubai, and London could be next 🚀 Curious for details? Check out The Future / Forbes interview with me

Why am I writing this blog?

We, Placy’s founders, believe that a good product should be created for a specific community, not for ourselves. You folks are our social partners, our alarm when we do wrong, our fun club when we do right. AI is still a land unknown. Welcome to the pioneering life where every insight is a treasure!
