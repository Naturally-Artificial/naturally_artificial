First time here? 👀 This is our startup diary of launching 😇Placy AI Real Estate Assistant, 6-month young project proudly part of Microsoft Startup Hub

Sergey Osipov, one of the founders, talks to you. I’ve co-founded number of businesses, including a proptech unicorn, promising myself to never start from scratch, but... Story about it

Placy is a free Assistant. Placy Pro is a SAAS b2b tool for real estate pros. So, in a nutshell, we build an automation tools for sellers/buyers and real estate agents (placy.ai)

Feel free to chat with 😇Placy MVP here 🤖 Only Cyprus property is available for buying at the current early stage (no rentals yet) — don't expect much, please, this is just a prototype

The official launch is set for July 3, at the RealtyON Expo in Limassol. Visit my speech there too, at the Speakers' Forum at 11:50

We're kicking off our pilot in Cyprus, followed by Greece, then the world 🚀 Here are some thoughts on our game plan. Curious for details? Check out The Future / Forbes interview with me

Why am I writing this blog?

We, Placy’s founders, believe that a good product should be created for a specific community, not for ourselves. You folks are our social partners, our alarm when we do wrong, our fun club when we do right. AI is still a land unknown. Welcome to the pioneering life where every insight is a treasure!
