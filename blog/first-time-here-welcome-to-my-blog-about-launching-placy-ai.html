First time here?

23 September 20241.93K views

First time here? 🧐 Welcome to my blog about launching Placy AI Real Estate Assistant, our amazing Startup of the Year, proudly part of the Microsoft Startup Hub. We just raised €1M in a pre-seed round, all set to shake the real estate industry with AI

Our core team are former colleagues from a large PropTech company that became a NYSE unicorn in 2021. I’m Sergey Osipov, co-founder of both—that past unicorn and our upcoming one :)

😇Placy Pro is a SaaS b2b tool for real estate pros. We’re kicking off the pilot in Cyprus, Athens, Dubai, and London. Request a demo: pro.placy.ai

😇Placy Assistant is an AI companion for sellers/buyers. It’s on the R&D stage. Curious for details? Check out The Future / Forbes interview with me, or watch the documentary filmed in my apartment this Autumn

Why am I writing this blog?

We, Placy’s founders, believe that a good product should be created for a specific community, not for ourselves. You folks are our social partners, our alarm when we do wrong, and our fun club when we do right. AI is still a land unknown. Welcome to the pioneering life where every insight is a treasure!
