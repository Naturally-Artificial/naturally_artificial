Hi, my name is Esther and I’m from the Compas AI team. We built AGT the first AI learning agent that helps you build a personalized learning plan using free content from across the web. So, you can learn new skills without paying for a course

Quick lessons we learned building it:
• Curating content > creating it. The internet already has enough, people just need direction
• Most learners drop off fast, structure and simple progress tracking changed everything
• Starting is hard, so AGT begins by asking your goal and level to guide you better

Community, try our AGT (for free) and I'd love to hear your feedback: @socialfifi on Telegram or feranmi@compasai.com
