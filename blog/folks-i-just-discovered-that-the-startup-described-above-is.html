Folks, I just discovered that the startup described above👆 is already live. And more than one!

Talent Recognition does candidate screening based on physiognomy. Difficult to match with behavioral patterns, plus, LLM bias, but the idea is not bad

Evidenced.app and Willo.video offer automated interviews with result interpretation

Reputy.io gathers insights from everywhere: video screenings, psychometric tests, etc. The goal? Validate core soft skills—leadership, communication, problem-solving, empathy. Think GitHub, but for soft skills. Сoaches love it. Why? It boosts their conversion rates: students land jobs faster or master critical skills better. For them, Reputy is both a sales tool and a PR asset. Fun fact: 89% of hiring mistakes happen because soft skills were misjudged

Helen Rantsevich, the founder of Reputy, saw my last post and shared this today. It’s not quite the mandatory [bank style] profiling app, but it’s close, and it makes sense too. Thanks, Hellen

Hey investors—Reputy has its first revenue and clients, and is raising!
