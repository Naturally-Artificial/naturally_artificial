Folks, I'm doing something new at BelTech this September.

06 July 20262.15K views

Folks, I'm doing something new at BelTech this September. We're recording a podcast episode — right at the conference. Not just the speakers. Everyone in the room participates. Full audience format, first time trying this :)

Come be a guest! Literally — if you're in the room, you're in the episode on Naturally Artificial YouTube. Think of what we did at the Warsaw hackathon, live and in front of 300 people

AI is breaking the logic of company building, so [agenda]:
• Do you still need a CTO on day one if AI writes the code?
• Headcount vs agentcount
• Which number actually matters?
• What's defensible when everyone has GPT-class models?
• Seat-based SaaS is dying. What replaces the pricing model?
• When a startup needs $50k instead of $500k — what happens to VC math?

Every point is a live debate now, but no consensus, right? Which makes for a good episode :)
