Folks, I’m looking for a co-founder to build an AI roll-up in Real Estate or HORECA

AI roll-up means taking a typical [slow-growing] business, plugging in AI to run most of the work that people do today, and turning it into something far more profitable and scalable. Then you buy up similar businesses and merge them into one platform. Scale

I'm a tech entrepreneur and I already have the AI ​​part [more or less] ready. A platform where AI agents handle the day-to-day operations instead of staff. It’s running for 50+ companies in our pilot market Cyprus/Greece. My team at Placy.ai can make a fork/branch of its AI platform. I can also fund the early stage

What I need is a partner: someone who already owns a company with a stable customer base in a country of 20M+ people. And who's bold enough to let AI agents actually run the biz

It might be Property Management (think Dwelly or Buena), a CRM or hotel-management something. What won't work: a standard buy/sell/rent real estate agency. No recurring client base means nothing to acquire when we scale. Why a customer base matters? The whole model depends on consolidation. You need existing, recurring clients to grow on top of. Number of investors (like General Catalyst or Zubr Capital) love and support this playbook

If this resonates, drop me a message. Please include a few basics about your business: number of clients, retention, ARR

The tech's ready. The capital's ready. You bring the clients, I bring the robots — we split the upside. Kind of :)
