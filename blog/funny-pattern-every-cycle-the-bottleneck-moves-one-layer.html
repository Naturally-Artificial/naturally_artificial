Funny pattern.

→ 2010: startups raised to hire devs
→ 2015: startups raised to rent AWS
→ 2020: startups raised to buy clicks
→ 2026: startups raise to buy tokens
→ 2030: startups raise to buy electricity? Data? Trust?
