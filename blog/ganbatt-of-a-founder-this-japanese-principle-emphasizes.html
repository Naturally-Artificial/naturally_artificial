“Ganbatté" of a founder

This Japanese principle emphasizes resilience in the face of difficulties, encouraging you to fight on despite the cost. Ganbatté is when you actually can't do anything. All your experience tells you that pushing on seems pointless. But you keep on trying

Nowadays, Japanese people have reduced using ganbatté to avoid adding pressure to those already stressed. Only children are still told ganbatté... and startuppers

Ganbatté or not ganbatté? — Hamlet, the Prince of Denmark, would ask from the stage, if he is an early-stage founder

Folks, what would your answer be?
