Google stock is down because of AI answers.

02 November 2026904 views

Google stock is down because of AI answers. Your web-traffic is down because of AI answers

Reddit’s CEO just said that Google still crawls Reddit. But AI Mode doesn’t send anyone back 😳

20 years of one deal. You write. Google sends people. You get paid

Now:
• Reddit lost 1M+ Google referrals per week in October
• AI Overviews cut clicks to the #1 result by 58% (Ahrefs)
• Reddit stock fell 29% on Friday. Alphabet lost $475B in a day

Google still needs the content. It just stopped paying for it with clicks

Reddit can lock content behind a login. A small publisher can’t… The Internet is entering a new era, yeah
