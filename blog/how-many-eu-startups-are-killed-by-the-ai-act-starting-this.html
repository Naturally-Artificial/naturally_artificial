☠️ How many EU startups are killed by the AI Act?

Starting this week, it’s no longer allowed to monitor emotions with AI, profile individuals with biometrics, and “manipulate” users with AI predictive tools. Fantastic tech just closed for European founders!

So, what’s still okay?
• Translating text on signs, menus, storefronts
• Identifying objects and landmarks
• Private, non-invasive personal use

The next wave of restrictions will come into play in August 🚫 [regulatory framework]

I can’t help but think back to when I got my Ray-Ban Meta AR glasses, which is an amazing product in the States but just a bulky piece of shit in Europe

If you voted for Apple's next EU meme, you have won:
“— Hey Siri, what am I seeing? — Oops, this feature isn’t available in your region” [the full story]

It’s not a meme anymore. It is our freaking reality
