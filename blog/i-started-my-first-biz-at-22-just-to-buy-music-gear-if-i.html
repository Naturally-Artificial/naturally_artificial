I started my first biz at 22—just to buy music gear.

19 March 20253.04K views

I started my first biz at 22—just to buy music gear. If I weren't an entrepreneur, I’d likely be a musician today. But here we are: still surrounded by guitars and building things from scratch. Late-night Zoom brainstorms feel like jamming in a global band :)

Since 2023, growing 😇Placy, and writing this blog about our journey in launching an AI PropTech startup

Our core team are former mates from a real estate marketplace that became a NYSE unicorn in 2021. We raised €1M pre-seed in 2024. Realtors love the product. First clients, first revenue. Ticked all the pre-seed boxes. Time to scale

Curious for details? Check out The Future / Forbes interview with me, or watch the documentary about AI in Europe filmed in my place. And yes, feel free to request a demo: pro.placy.ai
