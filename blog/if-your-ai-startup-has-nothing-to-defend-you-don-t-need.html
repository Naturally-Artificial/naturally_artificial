If your AI startup has nothing to defend, you don’t need moats!

Investors love to ask founders “What are your moats?” mainly meaning that some next Anthropic or OpenAI update can wipe out your “GPT wrapper,” leaving nothing to return investments

Indeed, the first question that I heard last year when raising pre-seed for Placy was "How is she different from ChatGPT?"

The first moat of a startup is speed. Then relationships with its users/clients, brand values, and so on…
