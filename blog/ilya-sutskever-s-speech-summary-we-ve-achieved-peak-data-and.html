Ilya Sutskever’s speech summary: “We’ve achieved peak data and…

15 December 20243.17K views

Ilya Sutskever’s speech summary:

“We’ve achieved peak data and there’ll be no more. We have to deal with the data that we have. There’s only one internet”

“Pre-training as we know it will unquestionably end”

AI systems will be both agentic and capable of reasoning. Unlike current pattern-matches AI, future systems will think step-by-step. But the more a system reasons, “the more unpredictable it becomes”

My personal take:
• everything (not just texts-n-pics) should be seen as data. Agents’ actions, strategies to achieve the goal, all kinds of synthetic things
• the time of x10 productivity growth has ended. Now it’s time to optimize algorithms and make money in the fields
