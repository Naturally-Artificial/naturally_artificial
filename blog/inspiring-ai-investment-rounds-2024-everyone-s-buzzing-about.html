🤑 Inspiring [AI] investment rounds 2024

Everyone's buzzing about $6bn to xAI or $1bn to Scale AI, right? I picked out a few smaller niche industry projects that could spark ideas for your startup:

$50M Poly AI is a voice AI for call centres which “resolves over 50% of calls”(OpenAI + own LLM)

$15M Anterior AI provides AI-powered dental care

$20M Orby AI corp agents for financial operations

$50M SmarterDx is clinical AI audit of diagnosis vs revenue. Boosts sales ($0,8M annually per 100 patients)

$50M Rad AI assists physicians/radiologists

$35M Sixfold AI logistics for supply chain optimization

$50M Legion is an AI automation for hourly employees

$9M Hazy AI for synthetic data re-engineering (this could relate to my idea of Instant Surveys, don’t you think?)

$225M Inflection AI builds personal assistants (Pi app)

$35M Praktika AI avatars to learn English (1,2M users, ARR $20M)

$30M Coactive AI structures the unstructured data for ML

$27M Witness AI controls security and governance for enterprise AI

$23M Orca AI stops ships from colliding

$15M Nory AI manages restaurants

Inspiring list, but remember about 90% of startups that failed to rise money
