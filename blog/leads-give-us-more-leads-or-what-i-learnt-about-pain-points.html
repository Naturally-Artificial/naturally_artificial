Leads! Give us more leads!!! Or what I learnt about pain points in real estate sales

This spring, the first real estate agencies signed up for 😇Placy AI tools. The stats showed over 30% growth in property viewing requests. Same incoming traffic, but a third more wins. Awesome, right?

So, we quickly decided that Placy addresses such a huge pain point: realtors don’t lose 30% of deals when Placy handles the calls, doing CRM work and all that

What a naive conclusion!!! Most realtors don’t care. Typical response:
— Oh, yes, this is a problem we’re losing lots of deals! We should definitely connect to your AI tools… But I’m busy this week… Let’s talk in 3 months

Difficult to believe, but productivity is not a pain point in real estate sales 🤯

Well, folks. But what do you really need?
— Leads! Just give us more leads!!!
