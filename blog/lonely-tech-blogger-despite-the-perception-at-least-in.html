Lonely tech blogger

Despite the perception (at least in Europe) that Telegram is mainly a Russian thing, stats show—surprise, surprise—India makes 3 times more downloads than Russia

Indians spend ~9 minutes a day on Telegram, mostly just exchanging messages. Whereas Russian speakers are on it for ~17 minutes, reading channels. Russian, Belarusian, Ukrainian media are paralyzed by propaganda. So, Telegram's become the platform for uncensored news and indie bloggers

There are tons of English channels with over 100k subs too, mostly News and Web3. But I couldn’t find any tech bloggers writing in English here, I mean individual influencers

I feel lonely 🗿

Folks, let’s share our knowledge sources: drop in comments tech/AI/startup bloggers that you are subscribed to. Not the news copy-paste channels, please, but actual English writing influencers running the show

Or give me your hearts ❤️ if I'm the only English-speaking tech blogger in your Telegram
