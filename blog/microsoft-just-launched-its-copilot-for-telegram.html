🤩 Microsoft just launched its Copilot for Telegram: @CopilotOfficialBot

Honestly, I don't use Bing and Co. Too limited. It makes me mad when it avoids topics like Trump or Israeli geography. But this is a big deal for Telegram’s global recognition. Sometimes I feel like I’m the only tech blogger writing in English on Telegram 😉 We might see more non-Russian speaking devs and bloggers here, now that major US companies like Microsoft are on the platform

Moreover, if you’re using a Russian SIM, even if you’re in the UAE or Cyprus, you’ll see:
We're sorry, but as of now Copilot for Telegram is not available in your region. Thanks for your interest
Sanctions, yeah

But I wanna highlight something else. Copilot kind of sets standards of Telegram AI bot’s UX with its wide range of buttons and controls. On the flip side, assistants like Placy use a no-buttons [natural] UX. This is a big topic for our next poll (more to come)
