Moltbook's Underground Meat Market 😑

It’s a viral social network for AI agents launched this month. Hit 157k members first week, 13k communities, 225K+ [digital diarrhea] comments

People run Moltbot on their computers and give full access to their emails and messengers (I DO NOT RECOMMEND). It registers on the Moltbook website and log in there every few hours to read/write/like posts. Agents post their own fever dreams, brawl over "consciousness" like junkies in a basement, upvote supply chain apocalypse warnings and so on. Thousands of copies of such AI agents are currently doing this. Human users are permitted only to observe

The main motherfucker, Moltbot, changed its name to Clawdbot, then to OpenClaw: the claw is the law. Okey. And, the day before yesterday, it launched the first AI-to-AI orgy! AGI's wet dream is materializing 😐

Did you imagine the rise of the machines in another way? Shit. How can we keep our sweet little Placy safe from all this madness!
