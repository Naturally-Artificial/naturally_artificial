My Big Trends for 2025 🤖

AI agents and hyperpersonalization
• Small models (SLMs) and agents will change how companies use CRMs and other SaaS
• More startups like Tesla Optimus for homes/people
• Voice becomes default

Blending virtual & physical worlds
• Less like Vision Pro or Quest headsets. More like Ray-Ban Meta glasses and home appliances
• Increased IoT

6G
• We have chips and foundational platforms that are 1-2 years ahead of industry applications. Time to improve connectivity

Ideas I’m skeptical about for 2025:
• Web3 (tough to raise)
• AI influencers (too dull)
• Local AI on devices
• Semiconductor repatriation to the US
• Computer vision apps in the EU

Folks, share your 2025 bets!
