My guest is Uri Levine: 2x unicorn founder, biz-school professor, author of Fall in Love with the Problem, Not the Solution. This documentary is a fantastic masterclass for early stage entrepreneurs & investors!

→ VCs are still pricing 2021 reality in a 2026 world. Pace changed. Costs changed. Founders changed
→ PMF isn’t a milestone. It’s a survival test with no scheduled exam date
→ Who apologizes when the algorithm ruins your life?
→ AI-maps & autonomous cars (Waze navigation app is one of the Uri’s unicorns)
→ Crisis as the default mode…

No fluff, no slides with clip art. Just real cases from one of the Mediterranean’s sharpest entrepreneurial minds. Watch our talk on Naturally Artificial YouTube 👈

To meet Uri, I drove to a mountain village, passing by Limassol dam. There have been scarce rains in Cyprus the last 3 years. The dam reservoir is empty. No water for the city. Cracked earth is warning about the challenging summer ahead. I flew a drone over it. The footage is in the episode. A metaphor. No reasons to prepare for the crisis. Why? Watch the video 👆
