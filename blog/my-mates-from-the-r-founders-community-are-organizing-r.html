My mates from the R-Founders community are organizing…

29 January 20261.82K views

My mates from the R-Founders community are organizing 🇦🇪R-Founders Day Dubai on February 6th. I can't be there, but I highly recommend it to you. 50 attendees. Solid biz program & networking

AI in operations, distribution when everyone copies you. Fundraising under the new 2026 rules. Discussions with those who haven’t drowned in all this

Event format:
• Morning: brunch moderated by Katya Kurasheva (R-Founders founder)
• Afternoon: business sessions
• Evening: R-Global Party at a stunning bar with a DJ, to solidify connections (you can buy a ticket for the party separately by applying on the website)

Additionally, event participants will have the opportunity to visit the XPANCEO lab and join networking breakfasts on February 7th and 8th

Who's already joining:
• Alex Shokurov (CEO, Kokoc Group)
• Alex Shokurov (Founder, XPANCEO)
• Stanislav Levykin (CEO & Co-Founder, muver.pro / The Meal Menu)
• Roman Ulyanov (Founder, Greeneration.ae; Managing Partner, Rem.House; ex-Investment Director, Prytek VC fund)
• Evgeny Bisovka (Co-founder & CEO, Saola)
• More participants on the website

R-Founders handles all the logistics. Lots of new connections are guaranteed
