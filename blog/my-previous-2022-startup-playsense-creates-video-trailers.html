My previous 2022 startup Playsense creates video-trailers for…

01 June 20252.27K views

My previous 2022 startup Playsense creates video-trailers for AAA games/movies/apps (Linkedin). For example, one of the works, a collaboration World of Tanks Blitz 😄 Deadmau5, won several ad festival awards this year

Sure, AI video gen is cool, but what I want to say: a significant amount of time is still spent on concept creation and pitching/selling to clients. They don't pay for random pretty videos that don't match their marketing messages. Humans are irreplaceable there

And AI doesn’t really help in social/viral magic that makes 1M+ views on YouTube

By the way, I still play Blitz from time to time since we launched it about 10 years ago in Wargaming :) Check out this fantastic project 😋
