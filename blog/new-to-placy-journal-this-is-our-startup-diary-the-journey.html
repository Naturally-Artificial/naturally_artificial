New to Placy Journal?

This is our startup diary🦄 the journey of launching Placy AI Real Estate Assistant. 3-month update is here. 4 months young now, our team become part of the Microsoft for Startups Founders Hub, here

Placy is a free Assistant. Placy Pro is a SAAS b2b tool for real estate pros. So, in a nutshell, we build a service for sellers/buyers and real estate agents (placy.ai)

April'24 sets the stage for our MVP in Cyprus, followed by tests in Greece and the US, then the world 🌍 Why Cyprus?

Sergey Osipov, one of the Placy’s founders, is talking to you. I’ve launched 20+ businesses, promising myself to never start from scratch, but... Story about it

My expertise is based on working on the boards of three leading real estate marketplaces in Russia, Belarus and Ukraine. You may ask why didn't I start another marketplace in Cyprus when I moved here 9 years ago? Answer: boring doing the same thing over and over again. What changed? Technologies. AI and Digital Signature are enabling the creation of a new generation of IT tools, and they are global. This is the unicorn scale. So now I'm a startuper again 😆

Why am I writing this blog?

We, Placy’s founders, believe that a good product should be created for a specific community, not for ourselves. You folks are our social partners, our alarm when we do wrong, our fun club when we do right. AI is still a land unknown. Welcome to the pioneering life where every insight is a treasure 💎
