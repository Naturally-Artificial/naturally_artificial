Offline is not dead for AI B2B! Are you skeptical?

I used to think offline events are just for those who never logged into Google Marketing Platform

😀Placy participated in three mid-sized expos in May in Limassol, Madrid, and Athens. And, you know, I like it! It’s so cool to meet your customers in person, to hear their stories, achievements, and dreams, to ask your custdev questions

And you can get 10-20 solid leads for your startup each expo day! Especially if you’re having fun like our team. Clients love funny IT partners ;)
