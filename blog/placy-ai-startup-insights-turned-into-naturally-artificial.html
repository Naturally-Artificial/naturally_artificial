Placy🦄 AI Startup Insights turned into Naturally Artificial by Sergey Osipov

Two years ago I started this channel with a modest plan: gather 100-200 people to test Placy’s prototype. The MVP lived in Telegram, so I launched my blog here

By April 2024 we were >500 when the MVP was shipped. I discovered, wow, I’m an influencer! 😮

Thanks to your feedback and the team’s courage,😇Placy got its first clients and revenue in 2025. PMF found in Cyprus and Greece. Spain didn't give in right away, and the romantic phase continues in 2026. Also scaling to the UAE and UK

Eventually, Placy grew into a pretty young lady. And every startup is powered by AI these days. I’ve got fewer secrets. Now I talk more about last-mile-distribution and other founders' findings rather than our own product. And I love chatting with smart assholes who actually build

So, I announce the launch of Naturally Artificial! 🚀

Founder-to-founder talks in the age of AI. Mission-driven strategies, AI-first roll-ups, fundraising, and the usual insights around the industrial revolution

It’s dual media: Telegram to read, YouTube to watch. Cross-linked

Official language is English. But if guests prefer Russian, Ukrainian, or Belarusian (that I understand too), I’m ok to let them. We’ll add AI dubbing & English subs

My channel just got a new name (as you see above). Vibe unchanged. It’s still me typing this, not a bot, don’t worry 😉

Mantra: every modern business is a media business. Let’s see where this goes
