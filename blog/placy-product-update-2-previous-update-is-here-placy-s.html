Placy Product Update 2 🌟
(previous update is here)

Placy's leveling up from R&D to closed alpha! We just welcomed the first dozen external users—our friends—to chat with her, testing areas:

🔸 Onboarding and Profiling: Placy should decode your real estate needs with a human touch
🔸 Tone of Voice/Terminology: She's friendly and professional, speaking English, Greek, and Russian, just like a local real estate pro (and 50+ languages at an okayish level)
🔸 Market Insights: Placy should know the Cyprus' property market, ready to compare prices vs locations and offer insights
🔸 Relevant Listings: If she finds listings that really match your criteria, the test is passed

Key Focus of this early stage: building a social bond. Placy should become a companion. So, the KPIs: retention on day 1, day 3, and day 7—yep, just like in gaming 🎮

Under Development:
🔹 Stats/Funnel dashboards
🔹 Feedback collection
🔹 Connecting new data sources, including local classifieds and real estate agency sites
🔹 Porting to WhatsApp

As planned, Placy’s alpha will come to you folks in April 🚀
