Placy’s character

It is now clear:

First chat. Placy profiles you (sell or buy, house or flat, budget etc) and she detects your psyche type (rational, introvert, impulsive etc)

Then Placy adapts, choosing the persona 🎭 that best resonates with your psyche for deeper engagement and retention

Insight 1💡Psyche is an integral part of the user’s profile
