Placy turns 1 today.

31 October 20242K views

Placy turns 1 today. Why the all-black? Born on Halloween! 🧙‍♀️

A year ago, after endless interviews with realtors, we finally shook hands and launched this startup. What we invented for the first-ever Placy birthday party:

Dark Realtors Summit welcomes you!

You’ve been summoned because you are one of the chosen few. This is your chance to meet lords of shady dealings and to mark the first birthday of Placy, the artificial princesses of ghostly realms of real estate

Agenda:
• Fireside Chat: “Hellish Contracts: Who’s Better at Deals – Realtors or the Devil?”
• Panel Discussion: “How to Prove to the Bank You’re Dead and Don’t Owe Your Mortgage Anymore”…

Startup budgets can’t stretch for big parties. So, it’ll be about 30 guests + kids, mostly Placy’s team and early adopters, at my apartment (shown in the video above) that we decorated a bit 🦇

We intentionally picked Halloween for the annual corp party—figured there’s no need to come up with new themes every year—efficiency hack 😀🕯

Happy birthday, Placy, and wishing you a spooktacular Halloween 🎃 Booo!
