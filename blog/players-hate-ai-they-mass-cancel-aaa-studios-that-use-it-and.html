Players hate AI.

20 June 20261.91K views

Players hate AI. They mass-cancel AAA studios that use it. And then Unreal Engine 5.8 drops AI right to the editor 🤷

Codex and Claude now generate whole cities, art-direct lighting, etc. Players lost their minds — “Every Unreal game needs an AI label now!!!"

Sure, kids. And do we label music for AI mastering? Films for AI color grading? Cofounder of Playsense says — "Creators are being abused by AI”, but… (watch on the 14th minute here)

You know, I just realized what it actually is: technophobia. A fear of a tool that got too visible to ignore. Just a freaking TECHNOPHOBIA!
