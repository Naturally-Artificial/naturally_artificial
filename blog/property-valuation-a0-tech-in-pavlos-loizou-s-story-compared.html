Property valuation A0 tech in Pavlos Loizou's story.

05 December 20243.44K views

2:14
Property valuation A0 tech in Pavlos Loizou's story. Compared to 2007, modern data platforms face two issues:
• Overgeneralization—algorithms oversimplify complex properties, missing unique selling points
• Landlords’ eternal heartbreak when the €XXX valuation doesn’t meet their castle-in-the-sky expectations—data insights miss the emotional state of users

Look, we can resolve the second problem by changing how the report is delivered

Now: we send a 30-page PDF landlords don’t read. They immediately open the page with €XXX estimated cost of their property, and… Disappointment, right?

💡 New approach: AI assistant mandatorily reads the report summary aloud first. Starts with reasoning, explains key factors—then shows the €XXX and makes PDF available to download. Takes 1 min, softens the blow, increases the acceptance rate
