September 10–13, Limassol.

05 August 20261.86K views

September 10–13, Limassol. R-Founders is running a four-day camp for founders and C-level people building for global markets. The business program sits inside a trip around the island

What's on:
• Panel — where founders are putting money over the next 2–3 years. Open Q&A
• Business cases from the inside. Real numbers, real decisions, the mistakes that never make it to a slide
• Team Challenge. Teams take apart someone else's actual case and pitch a plan
• Fuckup Nights on a wild beach. Lost companies, lost money, lost teams

Between sessions: yacht races, an olive grove with oil tasting, a day at Vlassides Winery, a visit to a local company, ancient ruins

Evenings: dinners with Cypriot wine, the local business community, the R-Founders Party on the shore. It closes with a sauna and Sunday brunch

4 days of work, a room full of founders who'll tell you what's wrong with your plan, and an island that makes it easy to stay. Cyprus is good at this, believe me :)
