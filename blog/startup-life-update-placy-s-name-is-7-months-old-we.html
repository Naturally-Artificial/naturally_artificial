Startup life update 🤖🚀

🔹 Placy's name is 7 months old. We registered Placy Ltd in January, launched the [free b2c] AI assistant prototype in April, and completed our first marketing tests in May. Currently, Placy [MVP] chats with 50+ users and sends 300+ messages daily

🔹 Essential infrastructure is set up (CRM, dashboards, knowledge base, etc.), so we can monitor Placy’s performance and funnel

🔹 We pitched Placy Pro at two offline events in May (kudos to Sergey Tsyptsyn and Pavlos Loizou) and have 10 companies eager to test her with their listings

🔹 Placy Ltd is fully operational, the team is properly contracted, and the first payroll was processed in May

🔹 Thanks to the Microsoft Startup Hub grant, we have the funds to finish the ongoing R&D phase, and I've begun polishing slides for the pre-seed round

🔹 You voted for Instagram 4 months ago, remember? Placy's got organically 150 followers on her LinkedIn and 130 on Instagram. Placy🦄 AI Startup Insights community hit 2.5k. Lovin' you all ❤️

🔹 Placy has gained some media attention, including the article in The Future / Forbes (kudos to Marios Roussos) and the review on Disruptor (kudos to Aleksei Podkletnov)

🔹 Our marketing team is gearing up for the N1 Cyprus Property Show, RealtyON Expo, scheduled for July 3. We're excited to welcome you to our booth and my talk at the Speakers Forum stage which starts at 11:30

Whoooh, 7 months behind… and this is just the beginning 🙃
