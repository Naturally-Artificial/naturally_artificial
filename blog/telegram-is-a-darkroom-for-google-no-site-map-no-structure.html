Telegram is a darkroom for Google.

10 December 20251.82K views

Telegram is a darkroom for Google. No site map, no structure and internal linking. Shitty SEO. Channel owners (including me) struggle. Nearly no organic growth here

But I found a service that creates a Google-friendly mirror of channels: TGPages. AI adds in the missing descriptions, so it feels like a real site. Just look at this: https://tgpages.com/placy_ai

SEO is slow violence, many months for the first results. I registered 3 weeks ago and already have my first organic subscriber (their dashboard above). Wow! Tiny miracle

I love this AI startup. Clear niche. Actual value (I hope). Be like TGPages!
