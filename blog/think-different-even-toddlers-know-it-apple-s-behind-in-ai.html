Think different!

Even toddlers know it: Apple’s behind in AI. No frontier model. No arm‑race fireworks with OpenAI & Co

Indeed Apple never chased the AI cloud war. They optimized for the thing no one notices until it matters: local intelligence. Unified memory. Neural Engines. Hardware & OS designed as one system

Result? Developers cluster Mac Minis instead of leasing GPUs by the hour. Compute cost now looks indie‑affordable

Apple didn’t lose the game. They were playing a different game the whole time

Apple’s motto “Think different” in action!
