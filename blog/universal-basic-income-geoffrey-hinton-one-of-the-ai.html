Universal Basic Income

Geoffrey Hinton, one of the AI pioneers even before AI Winter, 2018 Turing Award (Nobel Prize for computing), just said on BBC that governments should roll out universal basic income to tackle the inequality brought on by AI. Kind of let’s tax AI companies and give the money to the people who will be replaced by AI

In other words, smart AI companies will finance lazy fools, right?

Drop a banana 🍌 if you support universal basic income, or a strawberry 🍓 if you think it’s a bad idea
