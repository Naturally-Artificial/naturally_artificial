Video enhancing apps are scams.

16 August 20242.37K views

Video enhancing apps are scams. They damage the reputation of genAI. The niche is free for your next startup

People saved tons of shitty videos from Nokia phones and VHS tapes. It would be fantastic to watch them in HD, right? Why has no one made a €9.99 app for this yet? 🙄

Exercising with existing videos seems a basic genAI task compared to the text-to-video magic of Sora or Runway. But surprise-surprise, it’s way more difficult. Just tested a few from Google’s first page

Costly pro tools like Topaz (€200) or Resolve Studio (€300) do upscaling, but not enhancing truly. Sounds like an idea for your next AI startup, folks
