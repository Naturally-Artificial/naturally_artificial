Vincent Vega just got back to California from a startup pitch in Europe:

— On the pitch meeting in Silicon Valley, you do the vision and disruption, and people get it, right? You understand the product, addressable market, all that… There in Europe, they wouldn’t know any brand vision, they ask for 10 years' financial projections!
— They don’t focus on vision?
— No, they got 6 months due diligence, than focus on KPIs and metrics.
— KPIs and metrics?!!! What do they call a unicorn?
— I don’t know. I didn’t go into a European VC that had one… But you know what they put on a term sheet in Germany? Term sheet signed by notaries!

Just brilliant 😂
