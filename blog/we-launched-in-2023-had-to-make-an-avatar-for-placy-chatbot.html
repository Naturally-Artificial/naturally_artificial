We launched in 2023.

19 December 20251.48K views

We launched in 2023. Had to make an avatar for Placy chatbot. We, founders, always listen to the voice of community, you folks. You voted for a photo‑real portrait of a young woman. So, Placy got a human face in February 2024

Frankly, I wanted a non-realistic image. Look at Claude, Gemini, ChatGPT’s abstract avatars. You disappointed me. And I did a tiny act of rebellion. I made 2D version (that smiling face) the channel’s logo. You see it at the top of the screen. Yes, this creamy graphic didn't become the company’s symbol, but it found its spot here. No, I don’t regret it 😏
