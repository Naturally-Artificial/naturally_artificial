Web Summit is coming!

Zubr (Placy's investor) is hosting an event for startup folks. Last year's party was 🔥 for networking—this year should be even hotter since I’m one of the "special guests" ;)

***

Zubr Capital gathers friends and partners to have a great time during the Web Summit conference in Lisbon!

This year's party theme is Insights from Successful IT Company Founders on AI: Leveraging Breakthrough Technology for Competitive Business. How can AI be effectively applied for business development? Let's talk to our speakers!

• Yuri Gursky, co-founder & CEO of Palta (Flo, Simple)
• Tanya Parfenyuk, CEO of Zing Coach, AI based fitness-coach
• Sergey Osipov, co-founder of Placy, Real Estate AI Startup

We are inviting founders and top managers of IT companies to join us for an enjoyable evening in a friendly atmosphere with a glass of wine and engaging conversation. Our guests will include founders of publicly-traded unicorn companies, top European companies, Private Equity and VC investors, and business angels

Event Details:
• Date: November 12, 2024
• Time: 7:30 PM – 10:00 PM
• Location: Lisbon, Portugal

What to Expect:
• Prime venue in the heart of Lisbon
• Wonderful networking opportunities
• 2-hour free bar

Online registration is here

Don't miss this opportunity to connect with industry leaders and gain valuable AI insights!
