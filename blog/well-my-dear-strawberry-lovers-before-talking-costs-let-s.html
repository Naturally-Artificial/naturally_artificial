Well, my dear strawberry lovers, before talking costs, let's clarify the dev strategy:

“GPT from the box” vs complex AI solutions

Shallow AI app: Like adding a shiny horn to a car. Louder, but fundamentally the same car. Example: a property portal with AI search. Quick, easy market entry. Good for some, but the changes are just surface-level. These startups don't challenge the existing paradigms, they just enhance them with AI. Shallow app, shallow innovations

Deep AI app: Built with AI's future in mind. Examples: computer vision based game, niche b2b AI assistant. Complex, takes time to design-n-test new paradigms, but it's about harnessing AI's full potential. A product that fits today and leads tomorrow

Invest in Deep AI apps 🌟🚀
