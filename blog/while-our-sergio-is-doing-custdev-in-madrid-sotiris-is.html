While our Sergio is doing custdev in Madrid, Sotiris is charming…

16 March 20252.27K views

While our Sergio is doing custdev in Madrid, Sotiris is charming Austin, Texas, with the first public pitch of Placy’s seed round. Here’s his report:

SXSW this year is absolutely fantastic (as always)! We joined three PropTech events and got a healthy dose of AI discussions. The mission was simple—meet forward-thinking tech folks, peek into what other PropTech startups are up to, and have real talks with US-based realtors to grasp the nuances of the local market firsthand

Placy was right in the thick of it. We joined the Proptech Council, the International House, and the International Accelerator—these guys are major players in turning Austin into a buzzing Tech Hub. Also attended the Berkshire Hathaway event and got some insights

Met so many incredible people, and I’m pretty sure a bunch of them are just as excited to jump on the Placy rocket. Austin, you are dear to my heart. I can't wait to be here again next year 🤘🤠🚀🎸

Placy’s CTO lives in London. CMO in Toronto. CEO in Limassol… If someone had told me this 5 years ago, I wouldn't have believed it. But this is how the shit is done in 2025 😎
