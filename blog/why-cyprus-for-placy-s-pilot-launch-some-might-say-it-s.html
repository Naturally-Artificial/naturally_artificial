🏝 Why Cyprus for Placy's pilot launch?

Some might say: it's because Os lives in Limassol 😁 Nope

We analyzed 20 countries and here is the real scoop for Cyprus:
🛠️ Low tech competition
🏘️ Fragmented real estate market
💰 Juicy commission rates (up to 10%!)
🌍 46% of deals by international buyers (hi, multilingual Placy)
📜 Legal/tax heaven for global biz + government perks for IT
Bonus point: Indeed, I’ve lived here for 9 years (lucky coincidence)

Small Island is good for a modest MVP marketing test budget. And our Cypriot team can easily handle Greece, testing the geo-expansion workflow. Then the global launch. That's the plan
