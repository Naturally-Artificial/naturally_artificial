Would you book a tooth appointment with an AI dentist? Why the instant “no”? 😏 Relax. We gonna talk about British dental clinics’ balance sheets, not your teeth

Dentist Dr. Randeep Singh Gill took a break from dentistry after burnout and a neck injury. He used that moment to rethink how dentistry can be run more efficiently in the age of AI. And he founded DentaCFO, an AI-powered co-pilot that democratises financial intelligence

Dr. Randeep also hosts TechDental Podcast. So, we recorded our founder-to-founder talk:
• Why people still don’t trust AI
• The 30% Problem: How AI Recovers Lost Leads and Missed Calls
• Real Estate Meets Dentistry: Parallels in Automation and Efficiency
• The Human in the Loop: empathy isn’t optional
• Data Discipline and the Future of Intelligent Practices
