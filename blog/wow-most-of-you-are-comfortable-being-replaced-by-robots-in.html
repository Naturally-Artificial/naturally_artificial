Wow, most of you are comfortable being replaced by robots in…

21 May 20243.29K views

Wow, most of you are comfortable being replaced by robots in exchange for social payments (see above). I really like the way Michael T. laid out the idea of Universal Basic Income in the comments to yesterday's post—so short-n-smart—I just repost it here:

* * *
We will inevitably end up with UBI due to AI and robotics. Machines can replace people at most of the roles but one — consuming. Robots don’t buy goods

You need to have customers with money to run the economy. So, I see the future like this:
- the government tax companies
- the government spread the money among all the people with UBI
- people continue consuming goods, paying more money to the most effective businesses

So, everything will be working pretty same way but with less labour.
Without UBI it will not be working at all
