Y Combinator’s ideas they want to fund this year:

1️⃣ Internal agent builders. Every employee builds their own AI coworker

2️⃣ Voice AI for email. Talk with your inbox (I’m skeptical, by the way. This niche is already taken by Gemini and Co)

3️⃣ AI tutors & personalized education tools

4️⃣ Healthcare AI admin waste automation

5️⃣ And, of course, full-stack AI companies! Don’t sell software to old-school clients. Replace them. Start your own firm staffed with AI agents

We talk a lot about AI startup moats, how to not be killed by foundational models, right? Maybe this is the answer: mine gold yourself instead of selling shovels to the miners! [related post]
