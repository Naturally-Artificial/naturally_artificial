You and a friend launch a startup. You split the pie 50/50 because it feels fair, right? Nope!

Reality check: less than half of founders did 50/50 in 2025

Three founders? Equal 33.3% shares show up in only 27.3% of cases. Four founders? You see the chart…

Moral: there are no sacred rules carved into early-stage marble. Equity follows contribution, leverage, timing, sweat (and ego). If you truly bring more—aim for more!

I just don’t get why this stat has changed so much for 3-4 founders in in the past year. Your thoughts in comments👇
