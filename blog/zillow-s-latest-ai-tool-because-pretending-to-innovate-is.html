Zillow’s latest AI tool: because pretending to innovate is easier

They just announced “A new AI-powered language-screening tool designed to identify discriminatory language from property listings”

In simple words, the biggest US real estate portal now uses AI to catch incorrect phrases about African-Americans and LGBTQ+

You guys seriously promote this technically banal feature with press releases?!! Cosmic scale disruption: to spot bad words 😂😂😂

Don't get me wrong, I totally support the idea of fair housing. But what stopped Zillow from developing this feature 5 years ago? A wrong words detector can be done without AI

Zillow continues: “This move aligns with the industry trend of using AI” — and this is the real message: We are an old legacy site. Let's at least pretend we follow AI trends

That’s all you need to know about the AI competitiveness of large portals
