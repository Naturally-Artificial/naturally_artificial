For guests

Tell it to someone who has been there

The host has built companies for thirty years — raised money, missed payroll, hired badly, shipped late. The conversation is founder to founder, which is why it goes where an interview doesn't.

01.Who we are looking for

A story only you can tell

Founders

Whose company changed because of AI — in how it is built, who it hires, what it sells.

Operators

Who have run the thing everyone else is theorising about: a roll-up, a round raised in the EU, a team moved across a border.

Investors

Early-stage, with a thesis and the scars that produced it.

02.What to expect

An hour, on camera, unhurried

The conversation

Long-form, 30–60 minutes, on video. What AI is actually changing for you — not what it might change one day.

Language

Mostly English. Some episodes are recorded in Russian and carry English subtitles or English dubbing.

Where it goes

YouTube and Spotify in full; cuts and clips on Instagram; announced and discussed on Telegram; posted on LinkedIn from the show's page and Sergey's own profile.

What you leave with

An episode that is supported on every channel at once, not uploaded and left to fend for itself.
