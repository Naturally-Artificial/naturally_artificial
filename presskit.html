01.Overview

About the show

Naturally Artificial is a long-form video show of founder-to-founder talks in the AI era. Host Sergey Osipov sits down with founders, operators and early-stage investors and asks what AI is actually changing in the way companies are built, financed and run. Episodes run 30–60 minutes, mostly in English, some in Russian with English subtitles or dubbing, and come out on YouTube and Spotify; the Telegram channel carries the writing in between

Format

Long-form video conversation, 30–60 minutes

Guests

Founders, operators, early-stage investors

Topics

Founder's stories, AI transformation in business and creativity

Language

Mostly English. Some in Russian, with English subtitles or English dubbing

03.The host

Sergey Osipov

Three lengths, to be quoted as they are. The full career timeline is on his page

In one line

Founder turned investor. Thirty years in his own startups, one of them a unicorn; now backing others and hosting Naturally Artificial

In fifty words

Sergey Osipov has built companies for thirty years — about twenty attempts, one of which became a unicorn. Today he invests in European early-stage founders across AI automation, AI roll-ups, MarTech, PropTech and AutoTech, and hosts Naturally Artificial, founder-to-founder talks in the AI era

The longer version

Sergey Osipov spent thirty years building companies of his own — a digital agency, a media business, a games studio, a PropTech startup — before crossing to the other side of the table. He raised money, missed payroll, hired badly and shipped late roughly twenty times over. One of those attempts became a unicorn

That history is what he brings to founders now: pattern recognition rather than a framework. Through Joox VC he invests at the early stage in Europe, concentrating on AI automation and AI roll-ups, MarTech, PropTech and AutoTech

He hosts Naturally Artificial, where founders talk to each other about AI disruption, raising capital, tech hubs and relocation — strategy for a game whose rules change mid-play
