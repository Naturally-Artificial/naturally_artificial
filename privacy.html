Privacy Policy

Last updated 29 July 2026

Who runs this site

This site belongs to Joox LTD, registered in the British Virgin Islands — the company behind Sergey Osipov's work and the Naturally Artificial show. If you have a question about anything on this page, write to os@placy.io.

What we collect

Two things, and only two.

What you type into the contact form: your name, your email address, your company if you choose to fill it in, and your message.

Anonymous visit statistics, but only if you agree to them. See the next section.

There are no accounts here, no newsletter sign-up and no advertising.

Cookies and the counter

We use Google Analytics to count visits and see which pages people actually read. It sets cookies in your browser to tell one visit from another.

It runs only if you press Accept on the notice that appears the first time you open the site. Press Decline and nothing loads: no cookies are set and no data goes to Google. Your answer is remembered in your browser's own storage so we don't ask again.

What we see is aggregate and anonymous — how many people came, which pages they opened, roughly which country they were in. Your IP address is shortened before it is recorded. We can't identify you from any of it, and we don't try to.

What we do with your message

It reaches us in two places: it is stored by Netlify, the company that hosts this site, and it is emailed to us so we can read and answer it. We use it to reply to you and nothing else. We do not sell it, rent it, or add you to any mailing list.

How long we keep it

Messages stay in our email and in Netlify until we clear them out. If you would like yours deleted sooner, ask us and we will remove it.

What loads from other companies

Some parts of the page are served by others, which means those companies can see your IP address and the fact that you opened the page. Their own privacy policies apply.

  • Google Fonts — the typeface the site is set in.
  • Google Analytics — the visit counter, and only after you accept.
  • YouTube — episode thumbnails load with the page. The video player itself loads only after you press play, and we use the no-cookie version of YouTube so nothing is stored until then.
  • Telegram — on the Telegram page, the photos and videos come straight from Telegram's servers.
  • Netlify — our host, which keeps ordinary server logs such as IP addresses and requested pages.

Where your data goes

We are registered in the British Virgin Islands, and the companies listed above are mostly based in the United States. So if you write to us from Europe or anywhere else, your message and the visit statistics are stored and read outside your own country.

Your rights

You can ask what we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. You can also withdraw your cookie consent at any time using the button above. Write to os@placy.io and we will sort it out.

Changes to this page

If anything here changes, we update this page and the date at the top.
